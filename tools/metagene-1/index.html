METAGENE-1

METAGENE-1 is a microbiome & metagenomics tool, first public in 2025, described in arXiv preprint (2025). 7B-parameter metagenomic foundation model pretrained on 1.5T+ base pairs of human-wastewater DNA/RNA for pathogen detection and biosurveillance.
