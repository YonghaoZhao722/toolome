Pyrite

Nov 2026

Genesis Therapeutics benchmark for protein-ligand pose ranking: 1,500 pairs of docked poses of the same complex (curated and random sets of 750), where the task is to pick the pose closer to the crystal binding mode. Each pose carries 3D ligand coordinates, an interaction fingerprint and physics/geometry descriptors (MM-GBSA, Mogul torsions, strain, PoseBusters), with a post-AlphaFold3-cutoff temporal split. The paper also reports LLM ranking agents (PyriteReasoner, PyriteFeaturizer).
