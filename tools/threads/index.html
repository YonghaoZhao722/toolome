THREADS

THREADS is an imaging & pathology tool whose code is published by mahmoodlab on GitHub, first public in 2026, described in Nature Cancer (2026). Mahmood lab slide encoder that turns CONCH v1.5 patch embeddings of H&E whole-slide images into 1,024-d slide embeddings, pretrained by aligning morphology with RNA-seq or DNA panel profiles.
