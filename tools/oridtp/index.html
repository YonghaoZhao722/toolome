ORIDTP

ORIDTP is a drug discovery & molecules tool whose code is published by TencentAI4S on GitHub, first public in 2026. AI pipeline for de novo design of high-potency dual-target therapeutic peptides: a conditional diffusion generator (PepDif), dual-target reinforcement-learning optimization (PepDRL) and a protein-peptide affinity predictor (PepDAF), demonstrated on GLP-1R/GCGR co-agonists and PD-1/CTLA-4 co-inhibitors.
