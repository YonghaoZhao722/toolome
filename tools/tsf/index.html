TSF

TSF is a clinical & biomedical llms tool whose code is published by YonghaoZhao722 on GitHub, first public in 2024, described in Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (2026). Transfer Survival Forest transfers Random Survival Forest trees from a large source cohort and fine-tunes them on a small target sample for colorectal cancer prognosis (gains down to n=50; preprint arXiv:2501.12421).
